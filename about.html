About Bonner Builders LLC

Master craftsmanship, personalized service, and a legacy of building homes that reflect the families who live in them.

Our Story

Founded by Paul Bonner, Bonner Builders LLC brings over 45 years of master-level carpentry experience to the Lakes Region of New Hampshire. Rooted in traditional building techniques and an unwavering commitment to quality, Paul and his team specialize in turning houses into personalized, custom homes built to stand the test of time.

From framing and finish work to full custom builds and renovations, every project is approached with the same care, precision, and pride that have defined Paul’s career from the very beginning.

Why homeowners choose Bonner Builders:
  • Over four decades of hands-on building experience
  • Attention to detail on every phase of the project
  • Custom solutions tailored to your home and lifestyle
  • A reputation built on trust, craftsmanship, and lasting results
